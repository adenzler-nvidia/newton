**Experimental:** Capture `SolverMuJoCo(use_mujoco_cpu=True)` steps of a CPU-device model in a Warp CPU graph with `wp.ScopedCapture(device="cpu")` and replay them with `wp.capture_launch()`, which removes the Python overhead of a step. Only plain steps can be captured; contact export, observables, masked updates, and `notify_model_changed()` raise during a capture.
