**Experimental:** Capture `SolverMuJoCo(use_mujoco_cpu=True)` steps of a CPU-device model in a Warp CPU graph with `wp.ScopedCapture(device="cpu")` and replay them with `wp.capture_launch()`, which removes the Python overhead of a step. Steps that export contacts or request contact or body observables, updates of selected worlds, `reset()`, and `notify_model_changed()` raise during a capture.
